# Privacy Policy for ClimaQ

**Effective Date:** June 25, 2026

Welcome to ClimaQ! Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our weather application ("App"). Please read this policy carefully.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide to Us

* **Feedback and Support Requests:** When you provide feedback or contact support, we collect the information you voluntarily share with us, including your email address and message content.

### 1.2 Information We Collect Automatically

* **Location Data:** With your permission, we collect precise location information to provide accurate, localized weather updates and forecasts. This information is used solely for delivering weather-related services and improving forecast accuracy.

* **Device Information:** We may collect information about your device, including device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and app version.

* **Usage Data:** We collect information about how you interact with the App, including features accessed, pages viewed, and time spent using the App.

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive weather-related and geolocation-related information from trusted third-party service providers to improve the functionality and accuracy of the App.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

* Provide accurate, real-time weather updates based on your location.
* Improve and personalize your experience within the App.
* Respond to your feedback, questions, and support requests.
* Analyze usage patterns to improve App performance and reliability.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations and regulations.

---

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

However, we may share information in the following circumstances:

* **Service Providers:** With trusted third-party vendors who help us operate, maintain, and improve the App, such as weather data providers, analytics services, and cloud hosting providers.

* **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

* **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or a portion of our business assets.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 5. Your Choices

### 5.1 Location Permissions

You can manage location permissions through your device settings at any time. Please note that disabling location access may limit certain weather-related features and reduce forecast accuracy.

### 5.2 Notifications

If the App provides weather alerts or notifications, you may disable them through your device or app settings.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

ClimaQ is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to delete it promptly.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the App and reflected by the updated Effective Date shown at the top of this document.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
